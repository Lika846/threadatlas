A: Fill the bottom third with coarse organic material (branches, leaves) if you want to save on soil, then a mix of roughly 60% topsoil, 30% compost and 10% something for drainage and structure, like coarse sand or perlite. For your two beds that's about 2.3 m³ in total. Top up with a few centimetres of compost every spring as the level sinks.

=== t163 | When to plant garlic | vegetables | - | en
U: when do i plant garlic in norway
A: In autumn, about 3–6 weeks before the ground freezes — usually late September to October in southern Norway. Plant cloves 5 cm deep, pointy end up, 15 cm apart, and cover with leaves or straw. They root before winter and you harvest in July.

=== t165 | Squash plants with white powder | vegetables | houseplants | en
U: White powdery coating on my courgette leaves, spreading fast.
A: That's powdery mildew, very common on courgettes late in summer. Remove the worst leaves, water at the base rather than over the leaves, and give the plants more space for airflow. A spray of 1 part milk to 9 parts water once a week slows it down. It rarely kills the plant before the season ends, so keep harvesting.

=== t166 | Monstera leaves turning yellow | houseplants | - | en
U: Lower leaves on my monstera are yellowing one after another.
A: The most common cause is overwatering. Check the soil 5 cm down: if it's still damp, wait. Water only when the top third of the pot is dry, and make sure the pot drains. One old leaf yellowing now and then is normal; several in a row with wet soil points to roots sitting in water. If the roots smell sour or are mushy, repot into fresh, airy soil and cut away the rotten parts.

=== t168 | Мошки в цветочных горшках | houseplants | - | ru
U: В квартире завелись мелкие чёрные мошки, летают вокруг цветов. Как избавиться?
A: Это сциариды (грибные комары). Их личинки живут в постоянно влажной земле. Что делать:
- дайте земле просохнуть на 2–3 см между поливами — это главное;
- повесьте жёлтые клеевые ловушки для взрослых мошек;
- насыпьте сверху слой песка или мелкого керамзита, чтобы самки не откладывали яйца;
- при сильном заражении пролейте землю препаратом на основе бактерий Bti.
Через 2–3 недели мошек обычно не остаётся.

=== t169 | Plants for a dark bathroom | houseplants | - | en
U: Which plants survive in a bathroom with a tiny window?
A: Snake plant, ZZ plant, pothos and cast-iron plant handle low light best, and ferns like the humidity if there's at least some daylight. With almost no light, nothing thrives long-term; rotate plants to a brighter room every few weeks, or add a small LED grow light on a timer.

=== t170 | Repot a cactus without getting stabbed | houseplants | - | en
U: How do I repot a big spiky cactus?
A: Wrap it in a folded newspaper or a strip of cardboard as a collar, and hold it with that. Use cactus soil with extra grit, a pot only slightly bigger than the root ball, and don't water for a week after repotting so damaged roots can heal.

=== t171 | Stueplanter når vi er på ferie | houseplants | - | no
U: Vi skal på tre ukers ferie i juli. Hvordan overlever plantene?
A: Samle dem i et rom uten direkte sol, vann godt rett før dere drar, og sett de største i en balje med et par centimeter vann. === t172 | Fiddle leaf fig dropping leaves | houseplants | - | en
U: My fiddle leaf fig drops a leaf every few days since I moved it.
A: Fiddle leaf figs hate being moved; leaf drop after a change of place is a stress reaction. Put it in the brightest spot you have without hot afternoon sun, away from drafts and radiators, and then leave it alone. Water only when the top few centimetres are dry. It usually settles within a month or two and starts new growth at the top.

=== t173 | Lawn full of moss | vegetables | - | en
U: Half our lawn is moss. How do I get grass back?
A: Moss wins where grass struggles: shade, compacted soil, low pH, or too much moisture. Rake out the moss in spring, aerate the soil, spread lime if a soil test shows it's acidic, and overseed with a shade-tolerant grass mix. Fertilise lightly and mow higher (5–6 cm). In deep shade, accept moss or plant ground cover instead.
