=== t453 | Rebuilding a bike's rear wheel | cycling | repairs | en
U: My rear wheel wobbles side to side after a pothole. Can I true it myself?
A: Small wobbles, yes. Flip the bike, use the brake pads or a zip tie on the frame as a reference, and find where the rim moves toward it. Loosen the spokes on that side and tighten the opposite side's spokes at that spot, a quarter turn at a time.
U: there's also a dent in the rim
A: A dent or flat spot can't be trued out. If it's small, the wheel may still be usable with rim brakes feeling a pulse; with disc brakes it matters less. A deep dent or crack means replacing the rim or wheel for safety.
U: replacing the wheel then, what do I need to know?
A: Match the axle type (quick-release or thru-axle and its width), the brake type (rim or disc, and the rotor mount), the freehub type for your cassette, and the tyre width your frame fits. A bike shop can match it in minutes if you bring the old wheel.
