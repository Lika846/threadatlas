=== t112 | Sore for days after leg day | strength | - | en
U: After my first leg day in years I can barely walk down the stairs, three days later.
A: That's DOMS, delayed onset muscle soreness, and it peaks 24–72 hours after unfamiliar exercise. Light movement helps more than rest: walking, an easy bike ride, gentle squats with no weight. It'll fade within a few more days, and the next session with the same exercises will hurt far less. Start the following weeks with fewer sets and build up.

=== t113 | Creatine — worth it? | strength | nutrition | en
U: Is creatine actually useful or just marketing?
A: It's one of the best-studied supplements and does work for strength training: a few percent more strength and a bit more muscle over months, mostly by letting you do slightly more work in each set. Take 3–5 g of creatine monohydrate daily, any time of day; loading phases aren't needed. You may gain 1–2 kg of water weight at first. Expensive versions offer nothing over plain monohydrate.

=== t114 | Commuting by bike in the rain | cycling | - | en
U: Starting to commute 12 km by bike. How do I not arrive soaked?
A: Fenders are the single biggest upgrade — full-length ones with a mudflap keep road spray off your back and feet. Add a breathable rain jacket, rain trousers or a rain cape, and overshoes for heavy days. Keep a change of socks and a towel at work. Panniers instead of a backpack mean less sweat on your back.

=== t116 | Велосипед скрипит при педалировании | cycling | - | ru
U: При каждом обороте педалей что-то скрипит. Где искать?
A: Чаще всего скрип от педалей или каретки. Проверяйте по порядку:
1. Снимите педали, смажьте резьбу и закрутите обратно.
2. Проверьте затяжку шатунов.
3. Смажьте цепь и посмотрите, не скрипит ли при этом.
4. Если не помогло — каретка: её нужно снять, почистить и смазать, а при износе заменить.
Попробуйте крутить педали стоя и сидя: если сидя скрипа нет, виновато седло или подседельный штырь.

=== t117 | Bike fit: knee pain on longer rides | cycling | - | en
U: After 40 km my knee hurts at the front. Bike is new.
A: Pain at the front of the knee usually means the saddle is too low or too far forward. With your heel on the pedal at the bottom of the stroke, your leg should be straight; then, with the ball of your foot on the pedal, you'll have a slight bend. Raise the saddle in small steps (5 mm), and check the cleats if you use clipless pedals — toes pointing the wrong way can twist the knee. A professional bike fit is worth it if you ride a lot.

=== t118 | First gravel ride | cycling | hiking | en
U: Planning a 60 km gravel ride with some forest roads. What should I bring?
A: For a ride that length: two bottles or a hydration pack, snacks every 45 minutes, a spare tube even if you run tubeless, a mini pump, tyre levers, a multitool with a chain breaker, a quick link, and your phone with an offline map. Tell someone your route. On loose gravel, lower tyre pressure (2–2.5 bar for 40 mm tyres) helps grip and comfort more than anything else.

=== t119 | Indoor trainer winter plan | cycling | - | en
U: I have a smart trainer for the winter. How do I stay motivated for 4 months?
A: Structure helps more than willpower. Pick a goal for spring (a sportive, a time), follow a plan with two short interval sessions and one longer easy ride per week, and keep sessions under an hour on weekdays. Zwift or similar apps help with group rides, and a fan pointed at you matters more than people expect — overheating is the main reason indoor rides feel awful.
